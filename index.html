UnHung

Monochrome & sculptural

One colour, everywhere. So the pack is the only thing you look at.

The tube is a pastel rainbow, which is a hard object to photograph — put it in a busy scene and it fights everything. So the scene became a single hue: the water, the backdrop, the shadows, the tablets, all one blush pink, with the label as the only colour in frame. Top-of-funnel brand content — no headline, no claim, no CTA. It earns the look through craft rather than copy.

Static ad set · fruit lager

Jubel

Real and tactile

Beer cut with real fruit, photographed like the fruit is real.

A brand at Tesco-and-Sainsbury's scale still running catalogue photography — sterile cut-outs on white, or a blurry crowd at a festival, and nothing in between. The set takes the empty middle: natural light, honest ingredients, the pack's own design crisp and legible, and one confident shadow. Every claim on these traces to a source on Jubel's own site.

Static ad set · baby gifts

Pure Baby

Editorial and still

Not a baby clothes shop. A baby gift shop.

The buyer is almost never the parent — it is the grandmother, the aunt, the godmother, buying under time pressure for someone else's baby. Free gift wrapping is the strongest thing they have and it had never once been photographed. That became the campaign.

Animated ad set · equestrian

Huntington Sport

Graphic and loud

A category of beige aspiration. So: not beige.

Equestrian advertising is uniformly models on immaculate greys in Tuscan sun. Huntington's riders are at wet British yards at 6am. Going loud was a differentiation play, not a taste preference — halftone, scanlines, chromatic split, all built in CSS so every effect is controlled rather than hoped for.

Payne Street

Documentary

A film cut from a photo library. No shoot, no crew, no budget line for either.

Payne Street had good stills and nothing moving. The film was built entirely from photographs they already owned — graded, moved, cut to a beat. It is the piece that opens this page because it is the one that most surprises people.
